Power estimates & uncertainty

Power measures expected performance in points against an average FBS team on a neutral field. A team at +10 is estimated to be ten points better. This ordering measures predicted strength, separately from what teams have earned.

Mean is the average estimate across posterior draws; median is the middle estimate. A 95% interval contains the middle 95% of plausible strengths. “P(top 12)” is the share of draws in which a team is among the twelve strongest; it is not its chance of making the playoff.

How these forecasts work

A posterior is the model’s updated picture after seeing the games. Each draw is one plausible set of team strengths, home advantages, and game variability. Comparing teams within the same draw keeps their uncertainty connected.

This forecasting model starts with prior expectations based on last season’s strength, roster talent, and preseason AP points. The coefficients are estimated from earlier seasons only. As games accumulate, the model learns how far teams have moved from those expectations. This information is used for predictions, not the merit rankings.

The fit blends 75% final scoring margin with 25% efficiency margin, derived from postgame win expectancy. When efficiency information is missing, that game uses its actual margin. The residual variability is rescaled afterward to forecast real score margins. Home advantage includes both a national effect and a school offset, informed by the preceding three seasons; neutral games get neither.

Win probabilities average a Student-t predictive distribution over the posterior. This allows unusually large margins and includes game noise. “Stronger team” probability only compares underlying strength, so it need not equal the chance of winning one game.

Fit details

Weekly refreshes reuse the saved game feeds. A changed set of forecasting inputs triggers one separate fit, with no leave-one-game-out refits. If checks fail, the previous forecast stays available. The preseason prior must be reviewed for each new season.

Historical forecast accuracy

Weekly forecasts were reconstructed using earlier games, with bowls and playoffs forecast from the regular season. Comparisons use the same games with available closing lines. The supplied backtest used two chains with 250 retained draws per chain; its reported strength R̂ ranged from 1.01 to 1.05. Model selection used these seasons, so this is not an independent final evaluation. The 2022 forecasts use the last-season prior because earlier talent and poll training data were unavailable.
