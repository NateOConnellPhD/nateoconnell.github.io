Redrawing cross-validation folds can change the selected penalty, fitted coefficients, and variables retained by the lasso, even when the dataset stays the same. This work studies that source of variation and develops diagnostics for the reproducibility of prediction-tuned models. It distinguishes stability across fold assignments from sampling uncertainty and from identifying the true set of relevant variables.
