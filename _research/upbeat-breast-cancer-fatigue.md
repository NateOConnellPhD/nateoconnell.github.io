UPBEAT (Understanding and Predicting Breast Cancer Events After Treatment) follows women receiving breast cancer treatment alongside a comparison group without cancer. This analysis examined fatigue through two years after treatment began, including its associations with depressive symptoms, perceived stress, and measures of cardiac function.
