Some ideas benefit from a little space. This blog has room for short observations, longer essays, and questions that are still taking shape.

## A thought, then a little context

An opening paragraph can introduce a question. The next can explain what made it interesting: a book, a conversation, a result, or something noticed in passing.

Posts can include **emphasis**, *an aside*, a list, a link, or an image with a meaningful description.

- Start with one observation.
- Add the context that makes it useful.
- Leave room for the next question.
