I’m Nathaniel (Nate) O’Connell, PhD, an Associate Professor in the [Department of Biostatistics and Data Science](https://school.wakehealth.edu/departments/biostatistics-and-data-science) at Wake Forest University School of Medicine. My research focuses on the intersection of machine learning and statistical inference, especially uncertainty quantification for prediction models and estimates of their performance.

Much of my methodological work is motivated by questions from biomedical collaborations and real-world issues I encounter routinely in my applied work. Over years of applied research, I have repeatedly encountered problems for which I could not find satisfactory answers. In collaborations involving machine learning and prediction, many of these questions concern uncertainty: how much confidence we can place in predictions and performance estimates, and how the choices made during an analysis affect the conclusions we draw.

My recent work on cross-validation and random forests brings together years of thinking about these problems and working toward answers. Going forward, I plan to devote more of my research to developing statistical methodology and theory, with my applied collaborations continuing to shape the problems I pursue. I co-lead [SOUP Lab](https://www.souplabstats.com/) with Jaime Speiser, focusing on prediction and statistical inference.

I earned my PhD in Biostatistics at the Medical University of South Carolina, where my dissertation focused on eliciting patient toxicity scores and applying them to Bayesian dose-finding trials. Adaptive trial design remains an active interest. My current work extends that foundation to designs that use repeated patient outcomes.

I also serve as Director of Biostatistics for the [ARISE Cancer Consortium](https://www.arisecancer.org/), a multisite pediatric oncology consortium across North and South Carolina. I lead biostatistics for studies across the consortium. My broader collaborative work spans cancer, cardiovascular disease, pediatrics, neurology, emergency medicine, and electronic health record research, and has contributed to more than 70 coauthored papers.

My professional service includes grant review for the NIH, Department of Defense, and ASCO’s Conquer Cancer Foundation, reviewing for more than 20 journals, and membership on the [Nature Medicine Statistical Advisory Panel](https://www.nature.com/nm/statistics-advisory-panel) in Neurology & Psychiatry.

On **Out-of-Bag Thoughts**, I write about technical ideas and other things I explore out of curiosity.
