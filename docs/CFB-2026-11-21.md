# Margin of Merit: November 21 model update

This is the production specification for every season and weekly cutoff. It
supersedes the earlier strength prior and frozen-proximity ordering. Source:
the owner's `MoM_update_2026-11-21` handoff and Modeling Framework.

## Strength and score model

For a nonneutral game, the expected home margin is
`theta_home - theta_away + h0 + u_host`; neutral games have no home effect.
FCS hosts use `h0`, without a school offset. Margins follow a Student-t with
`sigma = 8 + exp(eta_sigma)`, `eta_sigma ~ Normal(log(6), .75²)`, and
`nu = 2 + exp(eta_nu)`, `eta_nu ~ Normal(log(5), .9²)`.

Current-season strengths are

```
theta_i = mu_division[i] + gamma_conference[i] + tau_division[i] * z_i
z_i ~ Normal(0, 1)
mu_FBS = 0
mu_FCS ~ Uniform(-infinity, infinity)
gamma_c = tau_conf * w_c
w_c ~ Normal(0, 1)
tau_FBS, tau_FCS, tau_conf ~ Uniform(0, infinity)
```

These are the specified improper flat hyperpriors, not wide finite uniform
approximations. Every conference gets the same prior. Independents have separate
one-team clusters. Season membership is frozen across the full fit and its
leave-one-game-out fits. Missing division or conference metadata stops fitting.
No historical strengths, polls, recruiting information, or conference bonuses
enter the strength model.

The national and FBS school home effects retain the correlated Gaussian prior
estimated from the actual preceding three seasons. Historical team effects are
season-specific nuisance parameters; their uncertainty is marginalized, and
national/school drift is added before updating with current-season results.
For 2026 the history is 2023–2025; each archived season uses its own rolling window.

Four sequential NumPyro NUTS chains use 1,000 warmup and 1,000 saved draws each,
target acceptance .90, and no MAP initialization. Exact deletion fits use the
same hierarchy, membership and historical home prior. Diagnostics include all
strength hyperparameters, conference effects, latent standard normals, home
effects, and residual parameters. Existing publication gates remain: rank R-hat
at most 1.01, bulk/tail ESS at least 400, zero divergences or tree-depth limit
hits, and chain BFMI at least .3.

If an exact deletion fit fails these checks, the runner makes one longer
attempt with 2,000 warmup and 2,000 saved draws per chain. Seed, model, priors,
target acceptance, tree depth and diagnostic thresholds stay unchanged. Both
attempts are logged, and no failed posterior is used. This is computational
recovery, not a change to the statistical specification or a convergence claim.

## Game scope and scoring

FBS–FBS, FBS–FCS, and FCS–FCS regular-season games contribute to fitting.
Conference title games count. FCS playoff games do not, even when the provider
labels them `regular`. FCS–FCS games marked by FCS Championship notes or playoff
competition metadata are retained in the exclusion audit, outside the likelihood.
Bowls and CFP games remain excluded. Original provider files are unchanged.

The 25th-strongest FBS reference, leave-one-game-out credit and SOS calculations
are unchanged. Bad PSIS diagnostics require exact deletion refits; there is no
fallback to uncorrected weights. The joint merit uncertainty still uses the
handoff's marginal-quantile transport approximation.

## Ordering

Start from posterior mean merit. Equal overall W–L records include FCS games.
A decisive direct series takes precedence over common opponents; a split
series never falls back to common opponents. Common-opponent dominance compares
win fractions against every shared FBS opponent, with a strict improvement
against at least one.

A comparison qualifies if opposing merit probability is strictly below .95
for head-to-head or .70 for common opponents, **or** its current rank distance
is at most two. Resolve the order, recheck proximity, permanently admit newly
nearby comparisons, and repeat until no comparison is added. `dynamic_trace`
records each admission and the ranks that caused it.

The revised handoff removes the undefeated-Power-Four rule entirely. An
undefeated team can rank below a team with two or more losses. Conference
membership supplies no ordering constraint; the estimated conference effects
in the statistical model remain unchanged. Maximize head-to-head,
common-opponent and merit-protection counts in that order; merit protection
uses probability at least .90.
Among objective-optimal orders, minimize global inversions of the raw order.
The existing exact subset/MILP solver implements this objective, including
unconstrained teams' placement; stable IDs break remaining exact ties. Python
and browser use the same rules. No scalar compromise weights or approximate
ordering fallback are introduced.

## Integration and refits

`margin-of-merit-2026-11-21` remains the fit generation. Models predating that
generation still require refitting; old-model draws cannot be relabeled as
current results. Conference membership participates in fit cache identity.
`margin-of-merit-2026-11-21-no-p4` identifies published rankings after the rule
removal. Every season/week must be regenerated with the revised ordering;
snapshots using the removed rule are excluded from the current archive until
replaced. The fit and LOO identities are unchanged by this policy-only revision,
so valid current-model posterior/merit arrays can be reused. The production
policy and reader controls have no switch to restore the removed rule.
The website reads saved JSON/CSV; visitors trigger no API calls or model fits.
Each snapshot has one official ranked table/CSV, plus separate raw-rank summaries.

The posterior explorer includes the FCS division mean, both within-conference
team spreads, the conference spread, and each FBS team's conference effect.
These distributions come from the actual saved joint posterior.

## Validation limits

The handoff's simulation and release results are supplied evidence, not fresh
production fits performed by this integration. Early-season behavior of these
flat hyperpriors remains unvalidated in the handoff. In particular, its proposed
readiness and prior-sensitivity studies must not be claimed complete on the
basis of source checks or a small sampler smoke test. A full current-season fit
and historical refits are separate jobs, subject to sampling and LOO gates.

Integration checks, repeated after the rule removal: the supplied 4,000 merit draws for 138 FBS teams reproduce
72 respected head-to-head results, 93 common-opponent results, the same
merit-protection optimum, and 82 raw-order inversions. Both dynamic admissions
match (Oklahoma over Ole Miss; Central Michigan over Massachusetts). Twenty-two
positions differ between equally optimal orders: production retains its stable-ID
final tie-break; the release's optimizer chooses a different tied solution.

Removal checks: 33 targeted policy, archive and publication tests passed,
including Python/JavaScript agreement for an 8–0 SEC team ranked below a
two-loss team and the same ordering when conference labels change. The offline
controller check confirms the removed control is unused and old-policy snapshots
are not displayed. No browser UI test, API request or model fit was run for this
ordering-only revision.

The targeted model trace, historical-home covariance/scoring, publication guards,
and offline browser-controller checks passed. A four-chain synthetic execution
check (100 warmup / 50 saved draws per chain) ran in 12.91 seconds with finite
draws and two divergences. This verifies execution only and would **not** pass
production convergence gates. It is not a timing estimate for a full-season fit.

The saved 2025 feed check excluded all 23 FCS playoff games (20 labeled regular season) and retained the SWAC championship. No provider API requests were made.
